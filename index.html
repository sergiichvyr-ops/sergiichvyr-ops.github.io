Become a beta tester

Help us get Tilecharm and Glidevane onto Google Play. It takes about two minutes to set up.

  1. Join the Google Group

    One group covers both games.

    Use a personal Gmail account, the same one that's on your phone. Work or school accounts are often blocked.

    Join the tester group

  2. Opt in on each game's testing page

    Use the same Google account you joined the group with.

    Install both games from Google Play, open them every few days, and stay opted in for at least 14 days. Google requires 14 days of closed testing before the games can launch publicly.
