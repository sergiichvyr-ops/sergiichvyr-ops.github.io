Glidevane

Tap gliders off the board in the right order. Vanes and portals bend the path.

About

Glidevane is a calm tap puzzle about clearing a night sky, one glider at a time.

Every glider points one way. Tap it and it flies off the board, as long as its path is clear. Tap a blocked glider and it bumps back, costing a heart. Plan the order of your taps, free the outer gliders first and untangle the board.

The twist: vanes and portals

  • Brass vanes spin clockwise or counter-clockwise and turn every glider that passes through, so routes bend around the board
  • Later levels add portals: a glider that enters the blue portal comes out of the orange one, keeping its heading
  • A short hands-on tutorial gets you started

Features

  • Thousands of levels, generated fresh and always solvable
  • Daily puzzle: a new board every day and a streak to keep going
  • Time Rush: replay cleared levels against the clock and earn up to 3 stars
  • Hints, undo and revives when you're stuck
  • No timer in the main levels — play at your own pace
  • Colour-blind friendly: every direction has its own shape
  • Plays offline · optional daily reminder

Free to play with ads. Remove ads with a single purchase. Optional reward videos give extra hearts and hints.
