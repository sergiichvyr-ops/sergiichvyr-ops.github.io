Tilecharm

Place jewel blocks, clear lines and stack lucky charms to beat the boss.

About

Tilecharm is a block puzzle with a roguelite twist. Drop pieces on an 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, and hit each round's target score. Between rounds, pick charms that multiply your points, reward gems and bend the rules.

Five slots, 30 charms to unlock, a boss rule in round 8, a shared Daily run, and 6 Heat levels when you want a real challenge. Short runs, no timers, no pressure.

Features

  • 8×8 board — clear rows and columns to hit the round target
  • Charms that multiply points, reward gems, and bend the rules
  • 5 charm slots and 30 charms to unlock
  • Boss rule in round 8
  • Shared Daily run
  • 6 Heat levels for a harder challenge
  • Short runs, no timers
